The air in this chamber hums with unseen forces. 

Books drift lazily through the air, their pages turning as if guided by invisible hands. 

At the center stands a dried fountain, long since abandoned, its stone cracked but eerily intact. 

A skeleton lies sprawled against the debris, its bony fingers still clutching a ruined tome.

As you step forward, a whisper coils around your ears—not spoken, but written into the air itself. 

The ink on the floating pages writhes, reshaping itself into a message:

"The guardian is awake."

Another page drifts past, its words fading as soon as you read them:

"It was never a beast of flesh, but one of gold. The vault’s first prisoner, its eternal warden."

The ink dissolves into nothing. The books stop turning their pages. The skeleton's jaw creaks open in silent warning.

The golden shell is not a relic. It is watching.
