"The golden vault of the Forty Thieves… a treasure trove beyond imagination, hidden deep beneath the desert sands."

Legends whisper of its wealth—mountains of gold, jeweled artifacts, and relics of forgotten empires. 

But few speak of the ones who entered and never returned.

You are a wandering magician, drawn by the lure of mystery rather than riches. 

The vault’s entrance revealed itself to you, as if it had been waiting.

Now, the doors have sealed shut behind you.

The thieves are long gone, their whispers echoing through the halls. 

Find the way out—before you become another legend lost to the vault.
